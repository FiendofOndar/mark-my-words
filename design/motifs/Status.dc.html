This canvas is the record, not the design

The current design lives in the Screens canvas. This one is how the theme got decided, so it deliberately still contains the options that lost. Four of the pages below are history and should look nothing like the app: they were built before the accent was settled, and repainting them would destroy the only reason to keep them.

Two things here were genuinely stale rather than archival, and both are fixed. The light theme kept a violet accent because it writes its colours in oklch and the sweep that moved everything to slate matched hex. And the pages were never labelled, so a superseded artboard and a live one sat side by side looking equally current.

What each page is

Current themeLive The feed, the detail screen and the check log as they should be built. Slate accent, glow at 20%.
Light themeUnfinished Hue now matches. The prose transparency still does not: alpha runs the other way on paper and needs its own pass.
Superseded — accent pickerHistory A Settings picker and two comparison sheets for a feature that no longer exists. Kept because the argument for killing it is in them.
How we got hereHistory The four original motifs and the two combined options. All amber, because amber was the accent when they were drawn.
