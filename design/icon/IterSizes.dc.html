Variations, at 150px

Full range

Same three colours, axis retargeted to where the strokes actually are. The violet is finally violet and the amber finally amber.

Lifted

Full range with every stop raised in lightness. The upper-left arc is the darkest part of the mark and the part sitting on the darkest ground.

Longer

A fourth stop, cyan, added ahead of the violet. More chromatic travel across the same distance.

Shorter

Two stops, violet into orchid, no warm end at all. The quiet version.

Grounded

Full range, with a faint indigo wash in the tile behind the cool end. The mark sits in the tile instead of floating on it.

Rimmed

Full range, flat tile, one hairline of light along the top edge and a shadow at the bottom. Physical without being glossy.

At 56px and 30px

On a home screen

Longer and Shorter are the two that change what the icon says. Longer buys vividness and starts to look like a spectrum, which is a crowded look. Shorter gives up the warm end entirely and reads more like software.
