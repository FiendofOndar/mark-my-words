Ink, repaired — and ink plus the red hand

Four tiles. The first is Inked with the missing hand put back and nothing else changed. The other three add the red minute hand.

The bug

The gradient was declared x1="0" y1="0" x2="1" y2="1" with no gradientUnits, so it defaulted to objectBoundingBox: every stroke got its own copy, scaled to its own box. The hour hand is a perfectly vertical line, so its box is zero pixels wide, and SVG declines to render a shape whose box collapses in that direction. The hand was never drawn. The repair is one attribute, gradientUnits="userSpaceOnUse", with the axis pinned to the bubble's own bounds so the sweep across the bubble is identical to what you liked.
