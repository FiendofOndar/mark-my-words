Refining the ramp

One variable each, all on the tile you picked. Nothing here changes the idea; they change where the ramp runs, how the hands sit on it, and how much of it survives at thumbnail size.

At 150px

As picked

The tile you chose, unchanged. Ramp across the mark, cream hour hand, red minute hand.

Ramp down the dial

The axis turned to run 12 to 6 instead of corner to corner. A clock's meaning goes around the face, and this is as close as a straight gradient gets.

Ramp around the dial

A real sweep from 12: green at the top, gold at 4, red at the bottom, returning through plum behind the tail. Android vector drawables support sweep gradients, so this is buildable.

Green hour hand

Hands in green and red rather than cream and red. The pass/fail pair becomes the two hands as well as the two ends of the ramp.

Brighter minute hand

The hand points into the red end of the ramp, so it is red on red. This lifts the hand's saturation to pull it off the rim behind it.

Heavier strokes

Same colours, bubble at 15 and hands at 14. Stroke weight decides more at 30px than any colour choice does.

On a home screen

The ramp holds up better here than the balanced red-and-green tiles did, and for a reason worth knowing: nothing in it depends on telling green from red. Someone who can separate the two reads a verdict; someone who cannot reads a warm gradient. It degrades into decoration rather than into nonsense.
