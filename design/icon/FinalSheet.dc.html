Rounded square

Android composites a 108dp foreground over a background and then lets the launcher mask the result; the manufacturer chooses the shape. The tail reaches almost to the corner of the square tile, which is right for the store icon and gets amputated by a round mask. The adaptive foreground is therefore scaled to 0.229 and re-centred on the composite's own bounding box, which puts the tail tip exactly on the 66dp safe circle. Both versions are exported; they are not the same drawing at two sizes.

Themed icon (Android 13+)

The system tints a single-colour drawable to the wallpaper palette, so the icon has to survive with no ramp at all. It does, because the structure is a bubble and two hands, not a colour scheme.
