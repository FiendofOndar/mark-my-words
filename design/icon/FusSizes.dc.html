Ink plus red hand

Quiet ground, colour in the mark, and the colour ends at the minute hand. Six readings of the same idea, ordered from the gradient doing all the work to the gradient doing none of it.

At 128px

Sweep

One gradient across the whole mark. Cool where the tail and the 12 are, red where the minute hand is. The red is not a separate colour, it is where the gradient lands.

Snap

The bubble stays cool and never reaches red. The minute hand is flat, saturated red. Soft meets hard.

Tip

The minute hand carries its own short gradient, out of the bubble's colour at the pivot and into red at the point. The gradient sharpens along the hand itself.

Focus

Radial, centred on the pivot. Red at the core, violet at the rim: the dial resolves inward.

Quiet

No gradient at all. Flat dusk, flat red hand. The control, and the one most likely to survive 30px.

Bloom

Sweep, with a faint red bloom in the ground behind the hand. Depth without a second colour.

At 56px and 30px — does the red still read

On a home screen

The red is the whole point of this round, so the small sizes are the test that decides it. A gradient that only turns red at the very tip of the hand has almost nothing left to show at 30px.
