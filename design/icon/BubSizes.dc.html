Bubble-clock, five finishes

Same structure throughout: a round speech bubble whose circle doubles as the dial. What changes is outline versus mass, stroke weight, and how much of the clock is spelled out. Shown at 96, 48 and 28px — 28 is the notification-tray size, where only mass survives.

The five

A · Outline, bold
Tail is part of the same closed path
B · Filled, dial cut out
Mass survives scaling better than line
C · Filled, hands only
The bubble's own edge is the dial
D · Outline, light
Elegant on a wordmark, at risk when small
E · Filled, amber hands
Amber is the one colour no user setting changes

Among neighbours, at 48px

A, C and E in the tray. If C holds at 28px and A does not, that settles it: filled wins, and the outline versions are for the wordmark and the splash screen where size is not the constraint.
