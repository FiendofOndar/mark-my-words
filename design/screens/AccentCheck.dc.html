Confirming the accent, once

Three fixed candidates on the screen you will look at most. Everything else on the canvas is already the first one. Green, red and gold are spoken for by the verdicts and amber is the attention flag, so the accent has to live somewhere between blue and violet — these are the three honest positions in that range.

Indigo is the safest and the most like every other app. Slate is the quietest and makes the glow nearly disappear, which costs the atmosphere the theme is built on. Violet is between them and is what the icon's ground already leans toward.
