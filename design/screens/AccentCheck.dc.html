Slate, and what it costs the glow

The accent is settled. The near-neutrality is the reason it is a good choice: nothing decorative can be mistaken for a verdict, and the amber ask is now the loudest thing on any screen that has one.

It does take something away, though, and the left-hand tile is what that looks like. Mixing a near-neutral accent into a near-black ground gives grey haze, not atmosphere, so the glow stops being light and starts looking like a dirty screen. The fix is to stop deriving the glow from the accent: it keeps a chroma floor of its own, bluer and stronger, while the accent stays neutral where it matters.
