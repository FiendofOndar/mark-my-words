The palette

Locked. Every colour below has one job, and the job is written next to it. If a new screen needs a colour, it takes one of these; if none of them fits, the screen is doing something the app has not decided how to say yet.

The accent

One value, and deliberately near-neutral, so nothing decorative can be mistaken for a verdict.

--color-accent#8792abPrimary buttons, active chips, selection. Never reading text.
--color-accent-inkoklch(0.44 0.045 265)The same hue, dark enough to be text on the light theme.

The glow

A tint of the accent, kept deliberately low. Every unit of glow is contrast taken off the ink on top of it.

--color-glowcolor-mix(#8792ab 20%)The ambient wash. Wide ellipse, origin near an edge, transparent by 78%.
--color-glow-softcolor-mix(#8792ab 12%)The same, behind something that already has a surface of its own.

Attention

Amber means one thing: the app is waiting on you. Nothing decorative is ever amber.

--color-attention#dba456Waiting on you, verdict-ready, dead link, Sample badge, mislabelled source.

Verdicts

Fixed and semantic. Every one is always paired with its word, never carried by colour alone.

--color-hit#5aa87aProven
--color-miss#cf6049Busted
--color-partial#d29a43Split
--color-ambiguous#8a8078Unclear
--color-voided#5d5650Moot
--color-late#c9a227Came true, after the deadline. A badge, never a verdict.
--color-draft#7a8fb8Draft. Nothing is running yet.

Ground and ink

The dark theme. The light theme redefines these names and nothing else.

--color-ground#131218The page.
--color-surface#1a181eCards and rows.
--color-rule#2e2a35Every divider.
--color-ink#f2ede3Titles, quotes, anything that has to be read first.
--color-ink-dim#a9a196Supporting values.
--color-ink-faint#736b61Labels, timestamps, hosts.

The mark

The icon's ramp. Fixed, and the only place these three appear together.

icon ramp start#5fbb8c12 o'clock on the dial.
icon ramp mid#ddb45eThrough the middle.
icon ramp end#d9533aThe tail, and the minute hand.
