Marketing

Rebuilt on the settled palette with the real icon. The earlier Box Score flyer predates every colour decision and is kept on the Directions canvas as history.

The claim that had to go

The old flyer said the app calls the verdict “the moment the deadline lands, day or night.” It does not, and it is built specifically not to. runPull has exactly one caller, reached from the feed refresh gesture or a detail screen’s Check now. No timer, no launch effect, no background service. Every check costs the user a metered API request, and on a free key there are twenty a day, so the cadence gate and the per-pull budget exist to stop exactly the behaviour that sentence was advertising.

What is true, and is what the new copy says: the app watches the clock and tells you when a call comes due, and when you do spend a check it searches for sources, fetches every page it cites, and confirms the quoted passage is on it before a verdict sticks. That is the harder and more interesting claim anyway.

Also changed

  • MISS is BUSTED, and the verdict chip is flat and filled rather than a rotated outline.
  • Citations show the host beside the publisher. The publisher is a name the model typed; the host is where the fetch landed, and two pages on one site once counted as two independent sources.
  • “66% hit rate” carries its five-call minimum on the label, because a rate printed without it is the cherry-pick the threshold exists to refuse.
  • Amber appears nowhere. On these pieces nothing is asking the reader for anything.
